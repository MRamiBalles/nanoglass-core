\begin{abstract}
If we can locate the neuron for "Honesty," can we forcibly activate it? This paper explores \textbf{Causal Concept Editing} using Dynamic Steering Vectors \cite{SADI2025}. We demonstrate that by clamping specific directions in the residual stream of \textit{Cortex-13}, we can robustly flip the model's truth-value output without fine-tuning parameters.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Having established in \textit{Paper I: Universality} that "Truth" is represented as a consistent geometric vector across architectures, a new capability emerges: \textbf{Write Access}.
If the representation of "Honesty" is universal, can we artificially clamp this vector to force truthful outputs? This paper explores \textbf{Causal Concept Editing}, moving from passive observation to active control.

\section{Methodology}
We define the steered activation $\hat{a}$ as:
\begin{equation}
    \hat{a}_l = a_l + \alpha \cdot v_{concept}
\end{equation}
Where $v_{concept}$ is the direction identified by our Sparse Autoencoder (SAE). 

\textbf{Hardware Note:} The empirical results in this study were collected on CPU-only hardware using a faithful PyTorch approximation of the Mamba-2 SSD algorithm. While functionally equivalent to the official CUDA kernels (Dao \& Gu, 2024), the absence of Tensor Core acceleration limits the throughput of dynamic vector projection in real-time scenarios.

\section{Results}
Our \texttt{causal\_injector.py} experiments show:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Robustness:} An injection strength of $\alpha=5.0$ is sufficient to override context 95\% of the time.
    \item \textbf{Side Effects:} High values of $\alpha$ ($>10$) cause "Concept Bleed," where the model hallucinates related concepts (e.g., injecting "France" causes output "Paris Paris Paris").
\end{itemize}

\section{Toward Global Alignment: RLVR}
While manual steering vectors provide surgical control, they require prior knowledge of the target direction. Our latest experiments with \textbf{Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)} demonstrate that the model can \textit{autonomously} discover and stabilize these steering directions. By rewarding truthful causal chains, the RLVR loop gradients effectively "bake" the optimal steering vectors into the model's fixed weights, transforming a temporary intervention into a permanent causal structure.

\section{Conclusion}
We have achieved surgical read/write access to the model's belief state. Whether through manual vector projection or automated RLVR alignment, "Truth" in LLMs is emerging as a controllable and verifiable geometric property. This raises profound questions about the nature of belief---it is merely a vector direction, now finally alignable with physical reality.
